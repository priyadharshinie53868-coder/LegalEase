# Phase 1 — Brainstorming & Ideation

## Project Name
**LegalEase — AI-Powered Legal Document Generator**

## 1. Problem Identification
Drafting legally structured documents (such as Employment Contracts, NDAs, Service Agreements, and Lease Agreements) is often intimidating, time-consuming, and error-prone for individuals, freelancers, and small business owners who lack formal legal drafting training.

Existing solutions either:
- Rely on rigid, outdated static templates that do not adapt to custom clauses or terms.
- Involve expensive legal consultations for standard preliminary drafts.

## 2. Proposed Solution
**LegalEase** leverages Generative Artificial Intelligence (Google Gemini) alongside FastAPI and Streamlit to allow users to input structured key terms and generate complete, context-aware, professionally formatted draft legal documents. Users can preview, edit the draft live in the browser, and export it into multiple industry-standard formats (**TXT**, **DOCX**, and **PDF**).

## 3. Target Audience
- **Small Businesses & Startups:** Rapidly generating standard offer letters, NDAs, and contractor agreements.
- **Freelancers & Consultants:** Creating clean service contracts and deliverables agreements.
- **Landlords & Tenants:** Drafting customizable residential and commercial lease terms.
- **General Public:** Understanding standard legal document structures before professional review.

## 4. Key Value Propositions
- **Speed & Efficiency:** Generates comprehensive drafts in seconds.
- **Customization:** Accepts custom clauses, parties, compensation, and dates.
- **Portability:** Direct export to editable Microsoft Word (`.docx`), print-ready PDF (`.pdf`), and plain text (`.txt`).
- **Transparency & Safety:** Clearly marks drafts as AI-assisted documents requiring human legal counsel review.
